OUR MISSION & VISION

Zero Food Waste. Affordable Dining. Thriving Planet.

According to global food studies, over one-third of all food produced globally is wasted, while millions struggle with food affordability. ZEWA (Zero Waste) was built as an engineering mini project to prove how a hyperlocal digital marketplace can eradicate unnecessary kitchen surplus.

🌍

Climate Action (SDG 12)

Food waste rotting in landfills generates 8-10% of global greenhouse emissions. We intercept food before it ever reaches a waste bin.

🤝

Community Equity

Students, young professionals, and families gain access to gourmet, nutrient-rich meals at 50% to 70% discounts.

🌱

Sustainable Business

Restaurants, bakeries, and cafes recoup ingredient and labor costs while enhancing their brand value as eco-conscious leaders.
