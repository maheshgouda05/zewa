THE PROBLEM & OUR SOLUTION

Why ZEWA Matters

Every day tons of safe, edible food is thrown away simply because it didn't sell by evening.

🍛

Safe Surplus Gets Wasted

Bakeries, tiffin centers, and hotels prepare fresh batches daily. What remains at closing time is clean and edible, yet gets discarded into landfills.

🤝

Affordable & Free Access

Students, working youth, and families get quality food at half price, while orphanages, ashrams, and hostels receive free meal donations.

🌱

Zero Complications

No payment gateways, no transaction commissions, no middlemen. Direct counter pickup and transparent verification keep everything simple.

OUR MISSION & VISION

Zero Food Waste. Affordable Dining. Thriving Planet.

According to global food studies, over one-third of all food produced is wasted while millions face rising food costs. ZEWA (Zero Waste) is a hyperlocal surplus food network connecting conscious dining establishments with neighborhood customers to eliminate food waste.

🌍

Climate Action (SDG 12)

Food waste rotting in landfills generates 8-10% of global greenhouse emissions. We intercept food before it ever reaches a waste bin.

🤝

Community Equity

Students, young professionals, and families gain access to gourmet, nutrient-rich meals at 50% to 70% discounts.

🌱

Sustainable Business

Restaurants, bakeries, and cafes recoup ingredient and labor costs while enhancing their brand value as eco-conscious leaders.

SUSTAINABLE TECHNOLOGY

Hyperlocal Circular Food Network Architecture

Hyperlocal Matching:

Real-time surplus inventory distribution within 5 km neighborhood radii

Food Safety Protocol:

Mandatory 2-4 hour pickup windows and strict culinary safety guidelines

Client-First Architecture:

Instant client-side caching, local data persistence & zero-latency execution
